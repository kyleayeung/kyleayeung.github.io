Kyle Yeung

Pianist, violinist, composer, and programmer.

I study computer science, economics, and music at Stanford. I build software, research how money moves through the arts, and perform and write music. Lately I've been working on tools that sit where those meet, like an app that listens to a practice session and tells you what to fix.

Music

Ten-plus years as a pianist and violinist. Principal pianist and later principal violinist of the Pacific Symphony Youth Orchestra.

I'm a Stanford freshman from Irvine, California, interested in software engineering, AI, and music technology. I'm currently exploring audio processing, web development, and applications of AI in music education.

Outside of engineering, I'm a classical pianist and composer. I also co-founded Practice Makes Perfect, a nonprofit that provides free music and coding lessons to underserved students through Illumination Foundation, a homeless shelter foundation.
